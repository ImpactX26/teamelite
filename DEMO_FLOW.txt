EDUGUIDE AI — UPDATED DEMO FLOW

1. Journey
   - Germany is the only destination.
   - Choose Study, Vocational Training, or Employment.

2. Profile Builder
   - Review education, experience, skills and languages.
   - Note the extra evidence categories: courses, internships, projects, certifications, awards and leadership.

3. Evidence & Documents
   - Upload core and extra evidence.
   - Add extra items with the supporting document filename.
   - Mark extra evidence verified to strengthen role fit / interview readiness.

4. Introduction Video
   - Analyze a short introduction video for background, motivation and career goals.

5. Qualification
   - Run the pathway-specific rule engine.
   - Resolve every hard blocker.
   - The applicant cannot proceed to Resume while mandatory evidence is unresolved.

6. Next Step
   - PASSED -> Verified Resume.
   - BLOCKED / NEEDS EVIDENCE -> reason-specific recovery plan.

7. Verified Resume
   - Core verified evidence + extra evidence sections.
   - Interview-readiness signal is shown separately from qualification.

8. Educaro Outreach
   - Applicant -> Educaro Deutschland GmbH -> matched company.
   - The applicant does not email companies directly.

9. Interview Monitor
   - Activate only after Educaro forwarding.
   - Simulate a company interview offer.
   - The UI extracts date/time and creates an applicant notification.
